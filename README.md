# Learn-Link
Student/Lecturer companion
## Introduction
Learn-Link is an educational support platform designed to help students learn. It supports collaborative learning and also constantly keeps the students updated on their academic life.
To the lecturer, it reduces the bulk of papers on is desk because students submit assignments online and he also sends the feedback online.
Equally, to the administration, Learn-Link is going to help them track both the students and lecturers progress.
